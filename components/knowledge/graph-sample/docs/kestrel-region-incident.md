# Cluster Larch region and incident

Cluster Larch runs in region Kestrel-1 and had a disk outage on 19 May. A firmware bug in one batch of drives caused a group of nodes to drop their volumes at the same time. The affected nodes were drained and the volumes rebuilt from replicas within two hours. The vendor published a fix, and the firmware was updated across the fleet over the following week. The incident is often confused with the March event in another region, but the two are unrelated.
