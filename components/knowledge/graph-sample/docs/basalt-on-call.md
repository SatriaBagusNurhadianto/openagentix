# Basalt on-call rota

Team Basalt rotates its on-call duty weekly, from Monday morning to the following Monday. Team Basalt's on-call engineer is Mira Okafor for the current quarter. She carries the shared pager and is expected to acknowledge alerts within ten minutes. Hand-over notes are written in the team wiki at the end of each rotation. Questions about the schedule go to the team lead rather than to the engineer on duty.
